Call Recording Retention & Destruction Policy

OPQ Enterprises (operator of TownSquare AI) · 4227 W Church St, Springfield, MO 65802 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how long TownSquare AI keeps call recordings, transcripts, messages, and related voice data, and how that data is destroyed when the retention period ends. It supports our commitments under state biometric and recording laws (including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act), our Privacy Policy, and our Data Processing Agreement.

Plain-English summary: call recordings, transcripts, and messages are kept on a rolling 90-day window and then automatically and permanently deleted. Businesses can delete individual records sooner. We do not use recordings or voice data to build voiceprints or to train general AI models, and we do not sell this data.

1. What this covers

  • Audio recordings of inbound and outbound calls handled by the AI receptionist.
  • Written transcripts of those calls.
  • SMS/text message content exchanged with a TownSquare number.
  • Call metadata (date, time, duration, direction, outcome).

A business using TownSquare may configure a shorter window, or delete an individual call from its call log at any time. Deleting a call removes our copy and instructs our voice provider to delete its copy.

3. No voiceprints, no AI-model training, no sale

TownSquare does not create or store biometric voiceprints or voice-identification templates from call audio. Recordings and transcripts are used only to deliver the service to the business that received the call (summaries, lead capture, call log). We do not use this data to train general AI/ML models, and we do not sell it.

4. How destruction happens

Deletion runs automatically on a scheduled basis against records older than the retention window. When a record is deleted, we remove it from our database and storage and instruct our telephony/voice sub-processors to delete their copies. Routine encrypted backups cycle out on their normal schedule, after which deleted data is no longer recoverable.

5. Copy on a device

The TownSquare Android app may keep an encrypted copy of the call log and transcripts on the business user's device so they are available offline. That copy is replaced each time the app syncs and is cleared when the user signs out.

6. Account closure

When a business's account ends, its data is deleted within 90 days, except where longer retention is required by law (for example, billing records for tax purposes) or to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud. Businesses can request deletion in-app under Account, or at our account deletion process.
