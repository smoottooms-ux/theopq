1. Program Description

OPQ Enterprises / TownSquare AI sends SMS to leads and customers who have opted in. Messages include: appointment confirmations and reminders, booking follow-ups, missed-call text-backs, and account/service notifications.

2. How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS by any of the following:

  • Completing the online SMS sign-up form and checking the consent box.
  • Providing verbal consent during an inbound phone call to the business.
  • Texting the business number first to start a conversation.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions (e.g., appointments and inquiries).

4. Cost

Standard message and data rates may apply. OPQ does not charge for the messages themselves; your mobile carrier's rates apply.

5. Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.

6. Help

7. Privacy

Your information is handled per our Privacy Policy. No mobile information or SMS consent data is shared with or sold to third parties for marketing.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
